Quantized delta arcs encoded as unsigned zigzag varints (base64); map units
retain their polygon/ring topology. ISO alpha-2 codes from source, except France
and Norway: Natural Earth ISO_A2_EH (FR and NO). Units lacking an unambiguous
code and disputed/indeterminate units are not auto-assigned. Natural Earth is
cartographic generalization, not a legal or surveying boundary. No inference
by nearest country; near competing borders and outside polygons are unknown.
